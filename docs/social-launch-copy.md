Hey HN,

We've been thinking about a fundamental problem with AI Agents: they're homeless.

Claude can write code. GPT can analyze data. OpenClaw can automate your workflows. But every agent has the same problem — close your laptop, and the agent dies. OAuth tokens expire. Sessions vanish. Next time, it starts from scratch.

AgentPuter gives your AI Agent its own cloud computer — a persistent runtime that never shuts down.

Think of it as: what a VPS is to a developer, AgentPuter is to an AI Agent.

Core ideas:

  - 24/7 Always-on: your agent runs in the cloud, keeps working while you sleep
  - Auth persistence: server-managed OAuth tokens with auto-refresh — no more re-login loops
  - Multi-agent support: run multiple agents in parallel without killing your local machine
  - Local-first privacy: data encrypted, stored in your controlled environment
  - Works with OpenClaw/ClawBot: one-click deployment of open-source AI assistants

The blog post dives deep into why we think "adding AI to software" is playing defense, while "building computers for agents" is playing offense. We look at the Claude Crash (Thomson Reuters -22%), Claude for Excel, and the OpenClaw explosion (153K GitHub stars) as signals.

But deployment is just Phase 1.

Here's the thing about OpenClaw (153K stars) — most users download it and don't know what to do next. The user base is still overwhelmingly technical. Powerful tool, but no bridge to everyday users.

That's where our long-term vision comes in: *Skills*. Our team comes from WPS AI (WPS Office serves 600M+ users), and we're betting big on Office Skills — reconstructing Office around agent-driven workflows that deliver deterministic results, not "try and hope for the best."

Imagine: instead of telling an agent "help me with this spreadsheet" and getting unpredictable output, you get a reliable, workflow-powered result every time. That's what Skills enable.

Our roadmap:

  Phase 1 (now): Solve deployment — give agents a 24/7 home (AgentPuter)
  Phase 2: Build the Skills ecosystem — starting with Office Skills
  Phase 3: Make OpenClaw truly usable by everyday users, not just developers

Our mission is democratizing AI agents — bridging the gap from "cool tech demo" to "tool everyone can use."

We're early stage — would love feedback from the HN community on both the architecture and this vision.

Early access: https://www.agentputer.com
